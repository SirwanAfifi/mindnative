Start with a thought.
Give it room.

Plan a project. Shape an outline. Find a connection. Make a map that helps you see the whole idea.

Connect and expand.

Add child ideas. Use cross-links to connect different branches. Move ideas as your plan changes.

Keep the detail close.

Add notes, tags, links, tasks, images, and attachments to an idea. Use the Navigator to find map content.

Change the view.

Use horizontal, vertical, radial, or compact layouts. Present one idea at a time when you share your map.

Take your work with you.

Import Markdown, OPML, FreeMind, and XMind files. Export maps as outlines, PNG, PDF, or SVG files.

Your maps.
Your files.

Save maps as .mindnative files on your Mac or in iCloud Drive. MindNative has no account system or advertisements.

A native Mac workflow.

Send selected ideas to Calendar or Reminders. Use Shortcuts to make maps and export outlines.

Optional Apple Intelligence.

Make ideas on a compatible Mac with macOS 26 or later. If Apple Intelligence cannot run, the app uses a built-in list.

Map content does not go to a MindNative server. Link previews contact the linked website. Update checks contact GitHub. Read the privacy policy.

Build from source.

A packaged app download is not yet available. Follow these steps to run MindNative on your Mac.

  1. Get the project.

    Run these commands in Terminal:

    git clone https://github.com/SirwanAfifi/mindnative.git
open mindnative/MindNative.xcodeproj

    You can also get the source ZIP and open MindNative.xcodeproj.

  2. Select the app.

    In Xcode, select the MindNative scheme and My Mac as the destination.

  3. Run MindNative.

    Select Product → Run. Make a document and add your first idea.

Before you start.

Is MindNative free?

Yes. There is no license fee. The MIT license permits personal and commercial use, changes, and sharing. Include the license notice when you share a copy.

Which Mac can run it?

The app targets macOS 13 or later on Apple silicon and Intel Macs. You need Xcode 26 or later to build the source. Apple Intelligence needs macOS 26 or later and a compatible Mac.

Do I need Apple Intelligence?

No. You can make and edit maps without it. When Apple Intelligence is unavailable, idea generation uses a built-in list.

How do I report a problem?

Open an issue on GitHub. Describe the steps and include your macOS and app versions. Do not include private map content.
